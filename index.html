The real issue

Most service businesses do not have a motivation problem. They have a clarity problem.

You try more content. More ads. More offers. More tools. Some of it works for a week. Then growth stalls again.

That is what happens when every problem gets treated like a marketing problem. Usually, one layer of the business is leaking and every other layer is paying for it.

We help you find that layer first. Then we fix one constraint at a time.

The framework

The 5-Layer Constraint Model

Growth gets easier when you know where the pressure is. We inspect five layers before prescribing anything.

01

Acquisition

Are enough qualified people entering the business?

02

Conversion

Are leads turning into booked calls, estimates, or sales?

03

Monetization

Are pricing, packaging, and follow-up creating enough value?

04

Retention

Do clients stay, return, refer, and expand?

05

Capacity

Can the team deliver without breaking the machine?
